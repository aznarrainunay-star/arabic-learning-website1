DRAWING GAME CONCEPT
====================

The student learns about the days of the week (الأيام) and activities associated with different days.

Instead of asking students to repeat a sentence, ask them to create a drawing related to a day and an activity.

There should be TWO activity modes.

---

## MODE A — DRAW A SPECIFIC DAY

The system randomly or intentionally selects a day and provides an activity prompt.

Example:

"يوم الاثنين"

"Senin"

Prompt:

"Bayangkan kegiatan yang biasanya dilakukan pada hari Senin."

Example context:

"Pada hari Senin ada upacara di sekolah."

Then display:

"🎨 Gambarkan siswa yang sedang mengikuti upacara di sekolah."

The student draws the scene on a large digital canvas.

The prompt should be accompanied by a simple visual reference or small illustration if appropriate.

The student should be free to interpret the scene creatively.

Possible prompts:

Monday:
"Upacara di sekolah"

Tuesday:
"Belajar di kelas"

Wednesday:
"Bermain bersama teman"

Thursday:
"Belajar bahasa Arab"

Friday:
"Beribadah atau kegiatan bersama keluarga"

Saturday:
"Bermain atau melakukan hobi"

Sunday:
"Berlibur bersama keluarga"

These are examples only. Keep the activity flexible.

---

## MODE B — MY FAVORITE DAY

Create a second option:

"🌟 Hari Favoritku"

Prompt:

"Pilih hari favoritmu dan gambarkan kegiatan yang kamu sukai pada hari itu!"

First, let the student choose a day:

الأحد
الاثنين
الثلاثاء
الأربعاء
الخميس
الجمعة
السبت

Then ask:

"ما يومك المفضل؟"

"What's your favorite day?"

After selecting the day, show:

"ما النشاط الذي تحب أن تفعله في هذا اليوم؟"

"What activity do you like to do on this day?"

The student can choose from suggested activities or create their own idea.

Suggested activities:

* bermain
* belajar
* membaca
* bermain bersama teman
* berkumpul dengan keluarga
* berolahraga
* pergi berlibur
* melakukan hobi

Also provide:

"✨ Gambarkan kegiatanmu!"

The student can then draw freely on the canvas.

DRAWING CANVAS
==============

Reuse the existing Arabic handwriting/drawing canvas component.

However, for this activity, make the canvas more suitable for creative drawing.

Include:

* large drawing area
* pencil/pen tool
* eraser
* undo
* redo
* clear
* optional brush size
* optional simple drawing tools
* submit button

Do not force students to produce a specific drawing.

The purpose is creative expression.

Allow students to draw:

* people
* school
* classroom
* family
* activities
* objects
* environments

Students should be able to use their stylus or finger on an iPad.

After submission, show an encouraging message:

"Hebat! Kamu sudah menyelesaikan gambar kegiatanmu! 🎨"

Do not grade artistic quality.

==================================================
ARABIC LEARNING CONNECTION
==========================

The drawing activity should still reinforce Arabic learning.

After students finish drawing, show a small language section:

"اكتب جملة عن صورتك!"

"Write one sentence about your picture!"

For example:

Selected day:
"الاثنين"

Student may write:

"أذهب إلى المدرسة يوم الاثنين."

Provide the Arabic sentence as an optional model.

Students can either:

1. copy the model sentence, or
2. create their own simple Arabic sentence.

Connect the drawing with:

* day vocabulary
* activity vocabulary
* simple Arabic sentences

This makes the drawing activity both creative and linguistically meaningful.
